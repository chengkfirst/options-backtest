## The decisions, in the order they block things

### 1 · The baseline, and the guard that cannot see it — read this before merging PR #3

**No baseline was updated. None needed to be, and that is the finding.**

`tools/baseline.py:109` builds its trades with `TradeTableEngine`, and
`backtest/engine/vectorized.py:111-115` **refuses a strategy with components outright**. So
the baseline configuration has never had a component and **no component change can ever move
it**. Both engine defects fixed on `fix/engine-state` are component changes. The guard whose
stated job is "an unintended change in results" is structurally blind to the entire class.

It was checked row for row anyway — 230 trades, net −11,822.29, identical in all three
measured states — and that agreement carries no information.

**What did move is in `docs/findings/baseline-change-2026-11-06.md`, trade by trade.** Every
moved trade exited **earlier** and **by a stop** — 5, 4 and 8 trades in three configurations,
with two `time_exit`s and three `expiry_otm`s becoming `stop_loss`es. P&L direction is mixed
(11 better, 6 worse), which is what tightening a stop by a fixed amount does.

**Your decision:** should `tools/baseline.py` grow a second configuration — event-driven, with
a stateful component — so this class of change is visible? Today it is not, and the next such
fix will also pass silently.

**refuse to replay**, with a message saying why. There is no such file in this repository
(`out/baseline/manifest.json` carries no component) — but one may exist outside it.
